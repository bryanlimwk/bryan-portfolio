01 / THE IDEA

Give candidates more than a careers page.

ShopBack Connect was conceived as an opportunity for fresh graduates to visit the ShopBack campus, meet employees, understand what working at the company could feel like, and receive practical career advice as they began their professional lives.

THE QUESTION BEHIND THE PROJECTWhat if early-career talent could experience ShopBack before interviewing here?

04 / WHAT IT BECAME

The format didn't stop with one audience.

The concept began with commercial talent and was subsequently expanded into engineering. After my time at ShopBack, I learned that the format was also extended into Product.

05 / WHAT IT TAUGHT ME
Recruiting has always been about more than filling the role.
ShopBack Connect gave me an early opportunity to work on the experience around recruiting: how people encounter a company, what they learn from its employees, and what value we can create before someone ever becomes a candidate or employee.

It also showed me how much I enjoy taking an ambiguous idea, aligning the people needed to make it happen, and owning the work all the way through delivery.
