RETROSPECTIVE RECONSTRUCTION
01 / THE IDEA

Give candidates more than a careers page.

ShopBack Connect started as an idea to bring fresh graduates into the workplace itself — to meet ShopBack employees, understand what life at the company felt like, and receive practical career advice from people already doing the work.

The first edition focused on commercial talent, before the format was later expanded into Engineering.

02 / MY ROLE

From concept to execution.

Concept

Came up with the ShopBack Connect idea and shaped the first event format.

Stakeholder pitch

Aligned internal stakeholders and built support for the experience.

Programme design

Planned the day, flow and the conversations attendees would move through.

Stakeholder prep

Prepared internal participants so they understood their role in the event.

Marketing

Worked with Design on the assets and handled event marketing and outreach.

Event delivery

Owned the execution and emceed the event on the day itself.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Built around conversations, not presentations.

The value of ShopBack Connect was the access it created: attendees could speak directly with ShopBackers, hear about different career paths, experience the office environment and ask the questions that rarely fit neatly into a job description.

04 / SHOPBACK CAMPUS

The workplace was part of the employer brand.

The event was held at ShopBack Campus — the office itself. That mattered because attendees weren't just hearing about ShopBack; they were experiencing the environment, meeting the people and seeing how teams interacted.

Recruiting has always been about more than filling the role.
