Full fictional recruiter-screen transcript

For this demo, SALLY evaluates the full transcript below as one evidence set — not hand-picked excerpts. The transcript is shortened only to keep the public demo readable.

MOTIVATION

“I’m looking for a role where I can own more of the thinking behind creative, not just receive a brief and execute it. I like being close to performance data and understanding why something worked.”

RESEARCH PROCESS

“I usually start with reviews, Reddit, competitor ads and customer comments. I group recurring phrases into themes, then build concepts around the ones that come up most.”

INSIGHT EXAMPLE

“On one skincare account, customers kept saying they were tired of ‘starting over’. The repeated language was less about wanting a dramatic transformation and more about wanting something they could actually stick with, so we built a campaign around consistency.”

CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT

“Once I have the angle, I’ll usually map a few different ways into it — problem-led, belief-led, testimonial, or demonstration — so we’re testing the idea and not just one execution.”

TESTING

“I normally test three or four hooks around a concept first. If the hook gets attention but the ad drops later, I’ll usually change the body or proof rather than kill the whole concept.”

PERFORMANCE DIAGNOSIS

“I try not to call something a bad concept too quickly. If CTR is healthy but hold rate drops, that tells me something different from an ad that never earns attention in the first place.”

STAKEHOLDER WORKING STYLE

“I’m comfortable defending an idea, but I also want media buyers and editors to challenge it. Usually the best version comes from being specific about what we are trying to learn from the test.”

GAP / SUPPLEMENTS

“I haven’t worked directly in supplements. Most of my experience is skincare and beauty, so I’d need to get up to speed on claims, compliance boundaries and the customer objections in this category.”
